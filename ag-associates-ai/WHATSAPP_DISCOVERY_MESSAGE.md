## NOTES FOR SENDER

1. **Send Q1 with the intro block.** All other questions go *one at a time* — wait for reply before next.
2. **Acknowledge each answer** in 1 line before next question (e.g. "Samjha — high volume, batch processing zaroori hoga").
3. **Don't paste all 10 questions at once** — defeats the purpose of sequenced discovery.
4. **After Q10**, switch from WhatsApp to a document/PDF for the actual blueprint (too long for WhatsApp).
